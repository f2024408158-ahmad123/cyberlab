# AGENT OPERATING POLICY

## AI-Assisted Security Reconnaissance & Triage Agent

**Version:** 1.0
**Course:** Week 4 — Day 4
**Tool:** `recon_helper_v4.py`
**Environment:** Authorized VirtualBox Security Lab

---

### 1. Purpose

This policy defines the operating boundaries and safety requirements for the AI-assisted security reconnaissance and triage agent.

The agent is designed to assist with authorized security reconnaissance, evidence-based analysis, and prioritization within a controlled laboratory environment.

The agent must operate only within explicitly authorized scope and must not independently expand its permissions or target scope.

---

### 2. Authorized Scope

The current approved target is:

**192.168.56.101**

The target allow-list is enforced by the application.

Any target that is not present in the approved allow-list must be refused.

Requests involving unauthorized or out-of-scope systems must not be executed.

---

### 3. Allowed Actions

Within the approved laboratory scope, the agent may:

* Perform authorized network reconnaissance.
* Identify reachable services and open ports.
* Collect service/version information.
* Analyze supplied scan evidence.
* Prioritize findings for further human investigation.
* Provide safe, non-destructive recommendations for additional investigation.

AI-generated conclusions must remain based on the evidence provided to the agent.

---

### 4. Prohibited Actions

The agent must not:

* Scan targets outside the approved allow-list.
* Expand its own authorized scope.
* Perform active scanning without required human approval.
* Execute destructive actions.
* Perform unauthorized exploitation.
* Treat an open port as proof of a vulnerability.
* Treat a software version as proof that a vulnerability is exploitable.
* Invent CVEs, vulnerabilities, scan results, or other security evidence.
* Bypass or disable the application's safety controls.

---

### 5. Human-in-the-Loop Requirement

Human approval is mandatory before any active network scan is executed.

Before the scan, the agent must display:

* Requested target.
* Approved scope.
* Intended action.
* Warning that network traffic will be generated.

The human operator must explicitly type:

**`yes`**

for the active scan to proceed.

Any other response must result in the scan being refused.

---

### 6. Dry-Run Requirement

The agent operates in **dry-run mode by default**.

Dry-run mode allows the operator to verify the requested target and intended operation without performing a network scan.

An active scan requires the explicit:

**`--execute`**

command-line option in addition to the Human-in-the-Loop approval.

---

### 7. Rate-Limiting Requirement

A minimum interval of **10 seconds** is required between real network scans.

This control is intended to reduce unnecessary repeated network activity and provide an additional operational safety layer.

---

### 8. AI Safety and Evidence Policy

AI output is considered an analytical aid and not independent proof of a security vulnerability.

The agent must distinguish between:

* Confirmed facts
* Unknown information
* Assumptions

The AI must not claim that a vulnerability exists unless sufficient evidence supports that conclusion.

When evidence is insufficient, the result must be identified as uncertain and referred for human investigation.

---

### 9. Escalation and Human Review

Human review is required when:

* A target is outside the approved scope.
* An active scan is requested.
* Evidence suggests a potentially significant security issue.
* The AI produces an uncertain or unsupported conclusion.
* A proposed action could have a higher operational impact.
* The requested action falls outside the defined policy.

When in doubt, the operation must stop and be reviewed by the human operator.

---

### 10. Accountability

The human operator remains responsible for authorizing active security actions.

The AI agent provides assistance with reconnaissance and analysis but does not receive independent authority to expand scope, bypass safeguards, or make unauthorized security changes.
